FAQ

Questions people actually have.

What exactly is a "session"? +
One message you send + one reply from Arjun = 1 session. Free trial gives you 5/day. A typical task: Arjun gives task (1), you clarify (2), paste output (3), get graded (4), make one fix (5). That's your full day. Resets midnight IST.
Do I need to pay for ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity? +
No. Free tiers are enough for all Day 1–Day 7 tasks. If you already have paid plans, great — but they are optional, not required.
What if I miss a day during the 7-day trial? +
Your account and progress stay safe. You can continue where you left off, but the free trial is calendar-based from signup date.
How is ZubhAi different from just chatting with AI directly? +
ZubhAi gives you structure: a role-specific plan, a daily task, grading, and a final publishable proof. It is coaching + accountability, not random prompting.
Can I use ZubhAi on mobile? +
Yes. The coaching chat and daily tasks work on mobile and desktop. For best speed while creating outputs, desktop is recommended.
What happens after Day 7? +
Your Proof Wall item stays. Upgrade to Pro/Max to continue with unlimited sessions, deeper tracks, and ongoing coaching.
