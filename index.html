How it works

5 steps to AI mastery

Simple enough to start in 5 minutes. Powerful enough to change your career.

01 /
🤖

AI Interviews You

Our onboarding agent asks you 5 quick questions about your job, goals, and experience level. Takes 3 minutes.

02 /
⚡

Get Your Challenge

Our AI generates a personalized task for your exact field — marketer, developer, student, teacher. In your language.

03 /
💬

Use the prompt we give you on ChatGPT or any AI tool. Work on it for 15–20 minutes. Then submit.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

🎯

Hyper-Personalized Challenges

Not generic AI prompts. Your challenge is generated for your exact job, skill level, and current progress. A nurse gets different tasks than a developer.

🔥

Streak System

6-day streaks with 1 optional rest day. Miss your streak? You lose it. That pressure makes you actually show up. Bonus points for working on rest days.

🤖

AI Grader Agent

Unlike courses with multiple-choice tests, our grader reads your actual output, evaluates depth and quality, and gives detailed feedback.

🌐

Your Language

Every challenge ends with your preferred language tag — Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil. ChatGPT responds in your language automatically.

🏆

Global Leaderboard

See where you rank globally and within your field. Top performers get early access to advanced modules and beta features.

💬

Community

Share your work, ask questions, see how others solved the same challenge. Learning alone is hard. Learning together is powerful.

🧠

AI Memory

Zubhai remembers your strengths and weak areas. Each new challenge is informed by everything you've done before. It adapts to you.

📊

Progress Dashboard

See your skill growth day by day. Know exactly which AI skills you've mastered and which need more work.

⚡

21-Day Skill Programs

Each program teaches one complete skill — AI for content creation, AI for data analysis, AI for coding. Finish in 21 days or take longer. Your pace.
