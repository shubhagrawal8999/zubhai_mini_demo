How it works

5 steps to AI mastery

Simple enough to start in 5 minutes. Powerful enough to change your career.

01 /🤖

AI Interviews You

Our onboarding agent asks 5 quick questions about your job, goals, and experience. Takes 3 minutes.

02 /⚡

Get Your Challenge

Our AI generates a task for your exact field — marketer, developer, student, teacher. In your language.

03 /💬

Do It on ChatGPT

Use the prompt we give you on ChatGPT. Work on it for 15–20 minutes, then submit your output.

04 /✅

AI Grades Your Work

Paste your output or share your ChatGPT link. Our grader reads it, scores quality 1–10, gives feedback.

05 /🏆

Level Up Daily

Maintain streaks, climb the leaderboard, complete 21 days and you've mastered a real AI skill.

∞ /🧠

It Gets Smarter

Every task updates your profile. Day 15 challenges are harder, more relevant, and built on what you've done.

Our story

Built from frustration.
Built for you.

I spent months watching AI courses, collecting certificates, feeling productive. But when someone asked me "can you build something with AI?" — I had nothing to show.
